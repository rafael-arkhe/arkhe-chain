security veto. Characteristics:
 Pure logic implementation (no software) : Immune to software, rmware,
or side-channel attacks.
 Deterministic veto with latency < 10 µs : Any anomalous condition gener-

 Independent power supply with backup capacitor : Ensures operation

 Immutable D1 Ledger logging of all veto events.
The Safety Enclave continuously monitors 47 critical signals across all subsystems
and has absolute authority to halt any ongoing operation. Once the veto is trig-
